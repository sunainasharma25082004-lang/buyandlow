Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 | Last Updated: October 31, 2026

Application Package: com.buylowindia.ecommerce

This Privacy Policy explains how BuyLow India ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and safeguards your personal data when you use our mobile application (BuyLow - com.buylowindia.ecommerce) and website (https://buylowindia.com).

1. Information We Collect

When you use BuyLow, we may collect the following categories of information to provide you with seamless e-commerce services:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, and password when you register or sign in via Google OAuth.
  • Order & Delivery Information: Delivery address, city, PIN code, and recipient contact number required to dispatch and deliver your ordered products.
  • Device Location (Optional): With your explicit runtime permission, the app accesses coarse or fine device location strictly to automatically fill in your shipping address for fast checkout. We do not track background location or use location for any advertising purposes.
  • Camera & Photo Library (Optional): With your runtime permission, used solely to allow you to upload images when writing product reviews.

2. Payment Information & Security

All online payments are securely processed by Razorpay, an RBI-authorized PCI-DSS compliant payment gateway supporting UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), Credit/Debit Cards, and Net Banking.

Important: BuyLow does not store or process your credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, net banking credentials, or UPI PINs on its servers. All sensitive financial data is transmitted directly and encrypted through Razorpay.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To process, pack, ship, and deliver physical orders to your designated address.
  • To manage your account, authentication, wishlist, and saved addresses.
  • To send you transactional notifications, order confirmations, and dispatch tracking details.
  • To detect and prevent unauthorized transactions or fraud.
  • To provide customer support and handle order queries or refunds.

4. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third parties or data brokers. Data is shared exclusively with verified service providers necessary to operate our services:

  • Razorpay: For secure payment gateway authorization.
  • Google Identity Services: For optional one-tap Google Sign-In.
  • Logistics & Delivery Partners: To fulfill and deliver orders to your shipping address.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion (Google Play Compliance)

We retain your personal data for as long as your account remains active or as required by Indian tax and accounting laws for purchase records.

How to Delete Your Account & Associated Data:
You can delete your account and profile data at any time directly inside the BuyLow mobile app: navigate to Account → Delete Account & Data. Alternatively, you can submit a deletion request by emailing us at support@buylowindia.com with your registered email ID. Your personal account data and saved addresses will be permanently deleted from our database within 48 hours.

6. Children's Privacy

BuyLow does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly remove such data.

7. Security Measures

We protect user data using modern industry-standard security protocols, including HTTPS (TLS) encryption for all network communications, salted bcrypt password hashing, and signed JSON Web Tokens (JWT) for secure session management.
